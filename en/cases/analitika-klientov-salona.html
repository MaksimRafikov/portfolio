Case · 01

AI beauty-salon manager

For salons booking in YClients: see who holds revenue, who is quietly leaving, and whom the front desk should call tomorrow — without Excel and without exporting the base.

Tool
live dashboard
Demo
synthetic M-Salon
Groups
5 segments

What I built

A management layer on top of YClients: clients fall into groups “core / newcomers / growing / at-risk / dormant”, each with one clear action. The owner sees the summary, the manager takes call lists, the front desk dials a short list. Salon data is read locally; public demo uses synthetic figures only.

What you get

  • Live dashboard with segments and weekly actions
  • Call lists, no-show report, Excel export
  • Install at the salon — without publishing the database online
  • YClients read-only; group labels optional

The online demo runs on fictional figures. Real client phones and the customer salon name are not published.
