Case · 02

Buyer contacts from tender platforms

For sales, manufacturing, and B2B suppliers: you need not “links to tenders”, but a working table — which company sought a product, its INN, email, tender link.

In Excel by niche
40,000+ rows
In the working database
100,000+ tenders
With email
≈ 64,000

What I built

Collected procurements from a platform, extracted contacts from the card, and additionally found company email by INN. If collection breaks — it resumes from the same place, without losing what was already found. Separate exports were also made for other client requests.

What you get

  • Excel: company, INN, industry, link, email
  • Two stages: platform + email lookup by INN
  • Can continue after an interruption
  • Tens of thousands of rows, ready for CRM import

Tables with live addresses are not published — only anonymized figures and the result format.
